# ============================================
# OVERVIEW
# ============================================

[Overview]
Bitsight is a global leader in cyber risk intelligence, providing security ratings, external attack surface management (EASM), third-party risk management (TPRM), and cyber threat intelligence (CTI). Its platform helps enterprises, insurers, investors, and government agencies make data-driven security decisions across their own digital ecosystems and their vendor networks. Bitsight is trusted by more than 3,500 organizations across 60+ countries.

[SummaryForLLM]
Bitsight delivers an integrated cyber risk intelligence platform combining EASM, TPRM, and CTI in a single data model. It provides continuous, outside-in monitoring of first-party and third-party cyber posture, security ratings with validated correlation to real-world breach likelihood, dark web and identity intelligence, and automated vendor risk workflows. Bitsight is the recognized leader in security ratings and TPRM, ranked a top performer by KuppingerCole and cited in Forrester and Gartner research.

# ============================================
# CORE PRODUCTS
# ============================================

[CoreProducts]

[External Attack Surface Management (EASM)](https://www.bitsight.com/solutions/external-attack-surface-management)
Continuously discovers, maps, and monitors an organization's full digital footprint — including cloud assets, shadow IT, and subsidiaries. Identifies exposed CVEs, misconfigurations, and risky open ports. Prioritizes findings by risk severity and supports remediation workflows via integrations with Jira, ServiceNow, and other tools. KuppingerCole ranked Bitsight a top performer in EASM in 2025.

[Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)](https://www.bitsight.com/solutions/third-party-risk-management)
Automates vendor onboarding, continuous monitoring, and risk tiering for third- and fourth-party ecosystems. Includes 60,000+ pre-populated vendor security assessments. Supports questionnaire management (SIG, CAIQ), zero-day response, and regulatory reporting. Forrester TEI study found a 75% reduction in time spent on vendor assessments and 3x ROI within six months.

[Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)](https://www.bitsight.com/solutions/cyber-threat-intelligence)
Delivers actionable intelligence from the clear, deep, and dark web — including compromised credential monitoring, adversary tracking, malware analysis, and vulnerability exploitation context. Powered by Cybersixgill, acquired by Bitsight to enrich EASM and TPRM with real-time threat data. Supports SOC, GRC, and threat hunting use cases.

[Security Ratings](https://www.bitsight.com/glossary/cybersecurity-ratings)
Outside-in, continuously updated scores (250–900 scale) that measure an organization's cybersecurity posture across risk vectors including patching cadence, open ports, web application security, DMARC configuration, and more. Used by insurers to price cyber risk, investors to assess portfolio exposure, and enterprises to benchmark their own and vendors' security performance.

[Exposure Management](https://www.bitsight.com/solutions/exposure-management)
Bitsight gives you visibility into threats and exposures across parties and through the cybercriminal underground, so you can see and protect your entire digital infrastructure—on-prem, in the cloud, and across the supply chain.

[Bitsight AI Capabilities](https://www.bitsight.com/blog/bitsight-iq-partnership-microsoft-threat-intelligence-briefing-agent)
AI-powered features including automated asset attribution, vulnerability prioritization, and partnership with Microsoft's Threat Intelligence Briefing Agent. Bitsight AI is embedded across EASM, TPRM, and CTI workflows.

 [Cyber Governance & Analytics] (https://www.bitsight.com/products/governance-and-analytics)
With Bitsight’s cyber risk intelligence solutions, you’ll get objective, evidence-based cyber risk metrics that have the strongest correlation to the likelihood of a cyber incident in the industry. Set the right targets, improve performance, and assure stakeholders that cyber risk is under control.

[UseCases]

[ManageYourOwnRisk]
CISOs and security teams use Bitsight EASM to continuously discover and reduce their own attack surface — identifying exposed assets, shadow IT, misconfigured cloud infrastructure, and unpatched vulnerabilities before attackers do.

[VendorRiskAndSupplyChain]
GRC and procurement teams use Bitsight TPRM to assess and continuously monitor third- and fourth-party vendors, respond to zero-day events, and meet regulatory requirements (NIS2, DORA, NERC CIP, SEC cybersecurity rules, ISO 27001, FISMA).

[CyberInsuranceUnderwriting]
Cyber insurers use Bitsight Security Ratings to assess applicant risk posture, price premiums, and monitor policyholders continuously. Bitsight is the industry standard for insurance diligence — partners include AIG and Moody's Analytics.

[InvestorAndBoardReporting]
Capital markets teams and boards use Bitsight data to assess portfolio cyber exposure, meet SEC disclosure requirements, and communicate risk to directors and institutional investors. Partners include Glass Lewis and Diligent.

[RegulatoryCompliance]
Bitsight supports compliance with NIS2, DORA, NERC CIP-013, SEC cybersecurity disclosure rules, APRA CPS 234, and other frameworks — providing evidence-based reporting and continuous third-party monitoring at scale.
